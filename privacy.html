Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Boundless doesn't collect your data.

Boundless is an independent infinite whiteboard app for iPad. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software kits, and it has no servers of its own. Your boards are files on your iPad, and they stay there unless you choose to export them or turn on iCloud Sync.

What Boundless accesses on your device

Your boards

Each board is saved as a file in Boundless's folder on your iPad, which you can see in the Files app. Your tools, colours and settings are saved in the app's own storage. Deleting the app removes them from the device.

Photos and files you add

When you insert an image, a file or a PDF, Boundless uses the system picker, so it only receives the items you choose. A copy is stored inside the board it was added to.

Face ID

If you lock a board, Boundless asks iOS to confirm it's you with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. iOS only tells Boundless whether the check passed. Boundless never sees or stores your face or fingerprint data.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, Boundless keeps your boards in your own iCloud Drive, using the Apple Account signed in on your device, so they stay the same on your other devices. Those copies are stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see them, and you can delete them from iCloud Drive at any time.

What we don't collect

We do not collect, store or transmit any personal information. That includes your name, email address, location, contacts, the contents of your boards, usage statistics, crash reports and device identifiers. Because Boundless has no servers and no analytics, there is nothing for us to see, share or sell. In App Store privacy terms, Boundless's answer is Data Not Collected.

Links

If you add a web link to a board and tap it, it opens in your browser. From there the website's own privacy policy applies.

Purchases

Boundless is free to use for 14 days. After that, a one-time unlock or a yearly plan keeps boards editable. Both are optional in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. Boundless only learns whether a purchase is active, through Apple's StoreKit, so it can unlock the app. We never receive your payment information.

Children

Boundless is not directed at children, and because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If Boundless ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
