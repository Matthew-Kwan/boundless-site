Why I made Boundless

I'm a lifelong learner, and I was tired of the options.

I take a lot of notes. Every app I tried for it on iPad let me down in one of the same few ways.

What kept going wrong

  • The price. Too many of the good ones cost far more than a place to write should.
  • Missing pieces. One had no whiteboard. Another couldn't mark up a PDF. I kept needing two or three apps to do one job.
  • Accounts. I didn't want to sign up for something just to write on my own iPad.
  • Lag. If the ink trails behind the Pencil, nothing else about the app matters.

What I wanted instead

I wanted something simple that had everything I needed, and that kept me completely inside my own data ecosystem: my device and my iCloud. No one else's servers, and no account in between me and my notes.

So I built it. Boundless is an endless page with drawing tools that keep up with the Pencil, whiteboards and PDF markup in the same app, and boards that are plain files on your iPad. If you turn on iCloud, they go to your iCloud and nowhere else.

Who it's for

Anyone who learns by writing things down: students, people teaching themselves something new, and people who just think better with a pen in hand. If that's you, I hope it's the app you were looking for too.
