An endless page for your Apple Pencil.

Boundless is an infinite whiteboard for iPad. Sketch, plan and think on a page that never runs out, with drawing tools that stay out of your way.

Coming soon to the App Store How it works

Free for 14 days with everything unlocked. No account needed. iPad, iPadOS 18 or later.

Drawing comes first

Everything else on the board is there to support what you draw.

Pencil

Pencil draws, fingers move

Your Pencil always draws with the tool you picked. Fingers pan, zoom and select. Tap with two fingers to undo and three to redo.

Tools

A small dock with the tools you use

Pen, pencil, marker, highlighter, straight lines with arrows, and an eraser. Save a tool and colour as a preset, and put the dock on any edge.

Dark mode

Dark paper that flips your ink

One button turns the page dark. Black ink becomes white and dark colours shift to stay readable, including what you have already drawn.

Beyond ink

Notes, shapes and connectors

Add sticky notes, text, shapes, images and links, then join them with connectors that follow as you move things around.

PDF

Mark up a PDF, or pin it to a board

Open a PDF as its own markup surface and export it with your notes, or drop its pages onto a board and keep working around them.

Scenes

Save spots and jump between them

Mark a part of the board as a scene, step through scenes with the arrows at the top, or present them full screen.

Your boards are files you own

Every board is a file on your iPad that you can see in the Files app, export and back up. Turn on iCloud Sync to keep them the same on all your devices, through your own iCloud Drive. There is no account, and nothing is sent to us.
